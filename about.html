About Thadingyut Festival

The Thadingyut Festival (also known as the Festival of Lights) is the second most prominent and joyous traditional festival in Myanmar, celebrated during the full moon day of Thadingyut (the seventh month of the Myanmar calendar). It commemorates the momentous occasion when Lord Buddha descended from the Tavatimsa Heaven after preaching the Abhidhamma to celestial beings, welcoming him back to the human realm with illuminated lights.
